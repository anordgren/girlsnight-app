Privacy policy

Effective October 25, 2026

What you say stays between friends.

Girls Night does not record your answers or conversations. Your game progress stays on your device. We use Firebase for app usage analytics and RevenueCat to manage purchases.

Your games

No account is needed. Game progress and settings stay on your device and may be included in Apple device backups. Girls Night does not provide cloud sync or record your answers and conversations.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand app use and improve Girls Night. It automatically collects usage and purchase activity, app and device information, an app instance identifier, and approximate location. Analytics is not used for advertising.

Purchases

Apple handles payments. RevenueCat manages deck access and restores using a generated customer identifier, purchase records, and app and device information. We do not send it your name or email address or receive your payment card details.

Service providers

Our providers are Apple for platform services and payments, Google Firebase for analytics, and RevenueCat for purchases. GitHub hosts this website and processes IP addresses and technical logs. The website has no added analytics or tracking cookies.

We do not sell personal information. We require providers processing data on our behalf to protect it to the same standard described here and required by law. They may process information abroad, including in the United States, with applicable transfer safeguards.

Storage and deletion

Deleting the app removes local progress and settings; copies may remain in backups. It does not automatically delete provider-held records. Analytics and purchase records are retained according to provider settings and legal needs, including restoring access and preventing fraud.

Contact and privacy

Girls Night is made by Nordgren Studio. If you email us, we use your address, message, and attachments to help you and keep them only as long as needed for support and legal obligations.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of your information, restrict or object to its use, or withdraw consent where it applies. Contact nordgrenstudio@gmail.com. We can handle requests only where we can identify the relevant records. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.
