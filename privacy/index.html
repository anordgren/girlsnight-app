Privacy policy

Effective October 25, 2026

What you say stays between friends.

Girls Night does not record your answers or conversations. Your game progress stays on your device. We use Firebase for app usage analytics and RevenueCat to manage purchases.

1. Who we are

Girls Night is provided by Nordgren Studio ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the Girls Night iOS app and this privacy website. Contact us at nordgrenstudio@gmail.com.

2. Your games and local settings

No account is required. The app stores your language preference, card order, current card position, completed decks, and rating-prompt history locally on your device. Girls Night does not provide its own cloud sync for this progress. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple backup settings.

The app does not ask you to enter answers or player names, and does not record audio, conversations, photos, or contacts. Your spoken answers are not sent to us or our service providers.

3. App usage analytics

Girls Night uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used and improve it. Analytics collection starts automatically when the app launches. It may process an app instance identifier, app and device information, language, interaction times, session information, and approximate location derived from network information.

Our usage events include which decks are opened, games started or completed, numbers of cards viewed, active play time, paywall views, and purchase or restore outcomes. Verified App Store purchase information is also used for revenue measurement. We do not include answers, conversations, player names, question text, or RevenueCat customer IDs in our custom analytics events.

Advertising storage, advertising user data, and ad personalization are disabled in the app's analytics configuration. The app does not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA), and Firebase's IDFV collection is disabled. Girls Night has no in-app analytics toggle.

4. Purchases and restoring access

Apple processes payments through the App Store. We do not receive your payment card details. RevenueCat helps us offer, validate, and restore the one-time purchase that unlocks all decks.

RevenueCat uses a generated anonymous customer identifier and processes purchase and transaction information, product identifiers, entitlement status, and technical app and device information. This identifier is pseudonymous; it does not mean that purchase records contain no personal data. We do not supply RevenueCat with your name or email address through the app.

5. If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and any message or attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond to you, resolve your request, and maintain necessary support records.

6. Service providers and sharing

We do not sell your personal information or use your answers for advertising. We use the following providers to operate the app and website:

  • Apple — App Store payments, purchase restoration, and platform services.
  • Google Firebase — app usage analytics and purchase measurement.
  • RevenueCat — purchase validation and entitlement management.
  • GitHub — hosting this website through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process visitor IP addresses and technical request information to operate and secure its service.

This website does not include our own analytics scripts, advertising, or tracking cookies. Service providers process information under their applicable terms and privacy safeguards. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect legal rights and security.

7. Retention and deletion

Local game progress and preferences remain on your device until the app's data is removed. Deleting the app, rather than offloading it, removes its local app data; copies may remain in device backups. Deleting the app does not cancel a purchase or automatically erase analytics, purchase records, or support correspondence held by service providers.

Analytics and purchase information is retained according to the providers' applicable settings and obligations. Purchase records may need to be retained to restore access, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations. We keep support messages only as long as reasonably needed to handle the request and any related legal or security needs.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal data, restrict or object to processing, and withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Contact nordgrenstudio@gmail.com to make a request. Because the app does not require an account, we may need additional information to locate records and verify your request.

You may also complain to your local data protection authority. In Sweden, this is Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY).

9. International processing

Our service providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States. Applicable contractual and legal safeguards govern those transfers.

10. Children

Girls Night is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The revised policy and its effective date will be published on this page.
