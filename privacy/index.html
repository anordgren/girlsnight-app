Privacy policy

Effective October 25, 2026

What you say stays between friends.

Girls Night does not record your answers or conversations. Your game progress stays on your device. We use Firebase for app usage analytics and RevenueCat to manage purchases.

Who we are

Girls Night is made by Nordgren Studio. This policy covers the app and this website. For privacy questions, email nordgrenstudio@gmail.com.

Your games and settings

You don’t need an account. Your game progress, language preference, and app settings are saved on your device and may be included in your Apple device backups. Girls Night does not provide cloud sync.

Your answers and conversations are never recorded or sent to us.

App analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how people use Girls Night and improve the app. It automatically collects usage and purchase activity, app and device information, an app instance identifier, and approximate location.

Your answers and conversations are never recorded. Analytics is not used for advertising.

Purchases

Apple handles payments through the App Store. We use RevenueCat to manage the one-time deck unlock and restore purchases. It processes a generated customer identifier, purchase records, and app and device information. We do not send it your name or email address, and we do not receive your payment card details.

When you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address, message, and any attachments to help with your request.

Our service providers

We do not sell your personal information. We use these services to run the app and website:

  • Apple — payments and App Store services.
  • Google Firebase — app analytics.
  • RevenueCat — purchases and restoring access.
  • GitHub — website hosting, including IP addresses and technical logs used to operate and secure the site.

This website has no added analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies. Providers handle information under their applicable terms and privacy safeguards. Information may also be shared when required by law.

Providers may process information in other countries, including the United States, with applicable safeguards for international transfers.

Keeping and deleting information

Deleting the app removes its local progress and settings. Copies may remain in device backups. This does not automatically delete analytics, purchase records, or support messages.

Analytics and purchase records are kept according to provider settings and legal requirements, including restoring purchases and preventing fraud. We keep support messages only as long as needed to help you and meet legal or security needs.

Your privacy choices

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, or restrict or object to its use. You can withdraw consent where processing relies on it. Email nordgrenstudio@gmail.com with your request. We may need information to find your records and verify the request.

You can also contact your local data protection authority, such as IMY in Sweden.

Children

Girls Night is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their personal information. Contact us if you believe this has happened.

Policy updates

If our data practices change, we’ll update this page and its effective date.
